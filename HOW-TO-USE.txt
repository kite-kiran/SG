HOW TO VIEW YOUR WEBSITE:
--------------------------
Just double-click "index.html" to open it in your browser.
Works in Chrome, Firefox, Edge.

HOW TO ADD YOUR LOGOS:
-----------------------
1. Copy your logo files into this folder
2. Rename the SG Gifts logo file to: logo-gifts.png
3. Rename the SG Mobiles logo file to: logo-mobile.png
The logos will automatically appear on the website.

HOW TO PUBLISH ONLINE (FREE):
-------------------------------
Option 1 - Netlify (Easiest, Free):
1. Go to netlify.com
2. Sign up with your Google/email
3. Click "Add new site" → "Deploy manually"
4. Drag and drop this entire folder onto the page
5. Your website goes live instantly!
6. You get a free URL like: sg-mobiles-gifts.netlify.app

Option 2 - Get a custom domain (.in):
1. Buy sgmobilesandgifts.in from GoDaddy or Namecheap (~₹700/year)
2. Connect it to your Netlify site (free instructions on Netlify)

HOW TO INSTALL AS APP ON ANDROID:
-----------------------------------
1. First host the website online (see above)
2. Open the website in Chrome on your Android phone
3. Tap the 3-dot menu → "Add to Home Screen"
4. It installs like an app — no Play Store needed!
